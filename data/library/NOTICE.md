# Attribution

Every article in this folder is content of the **SCP Wiki**
(<https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com>), licensed under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 licence (<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/>).
Each article belongs to its author(s): the author's name (as given by the wiki's
page history) is stored next to the text in `cNN.json` and shown by the game
with every article, together with a link to the original page.

## What was changed

- The page HTML was converted to plain text: headings, lists and tables were
  flattened; images, image captions, page navigation, rating widgets and
  collapsible/credit-box toggle links were removed.
- Article names (the part after "SCP-173 - ") come from the wiki's series pages.
- `meta.json` holds only derived facts: number, name, object class (from the
  page's tags or its "Object Class" line), rating, and which other SCP articles
  the article's text names.
- No wording of any article was edited, translated or written by this project.

## Sources

- Article text and metadata: the public SCP data dump at
  <https://scp-data.tedivm.com> (series 1 to 5).
- Names: <https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-series> and the following series
  pages.

The dump covers series 1-5 (SCP-002 to SCP-4999). Articles that were deleted or
replaced by a removal notice were left out.
